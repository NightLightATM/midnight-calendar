Midnight Calendar

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Midnight Calendar is a personal reminder app. It is designed so that your data stays with you: on your own device and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. There is no company server collecting your information.

Who runs this app

Midnight Calendar is an independent, non-commercial project. In this policy, "we" and "the app" refer to the Midnight Calendar web application. You can reach the developer at amproductionsdev@gmail.com.

What data the app handles

  • Your reminders - the titles, dates, times, and completion status you enter.
  • App settings - such as your chosen theme and whether notifications are enabled.

The app does not ask for, collect, or store your name, contacts, location, browsing activity, or any analytics.

Where your data is stored

  • On your device. Reminders are saved locally in your browser's storage so the app works offline. This data never leaves your device unless you enable Google Drive sync.
  • In your own Google Drive (optional). If you sign in with Google, your reminders are saved to a single file named midnight-calendar.json inside your personal Google Drive. This lets the same list appear on your other devices.

How the app uses Google account access

When you sign in with Google, the app requests only the drive.file scope. This scope lets the app see and manage only the files it creates itself - in this case, its one reminder file. The app cannot see, open, or access any other file, folder, or content in your Google Drive.

Midnight Calendar's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your Google data is used only to store and sync your reminders, is never transferred to anyone else, and is never used for advertising.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party. Your reminders move only between your device and your own Google Drive account. Communication with Google's servers happens directly from your browser.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows reminder alerts on your device. These are generated locally by the app in your browser; no notification content is sent to us or to any external service.

Your control and choices

  • You can edit or delete any reminder in the app at any time.
  • You can sign out of Google Drive from the app's settings, which stops syncing.
  • You can delete the midnight-calendar.json file from your Google Drive at any time.
  • You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Clearing your browser's site data removes the app's local copy of your reminders on that device.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
